# Fish label grid verification

Daily values are modes of nonmissing numeric weights. Ties use the smallest value. Intervals include both grid endpoints when observed; increments divide weight differences by actual calendar-day gaps. No unobserved dates are filled. Deviations at or below 1e-6 g/day are treated as decimal representation noise. A constant stretch is compatible with linear interpolation but does not prove sampling occurred.

## Additional grid exceptions

Ponds 10 and 12 have no observations on the specified grid dates. Their date-only records from October onward lie one day after that grid (2021-10-10, 2021-10-24, and so on), so those records support a shifted schedule rather than the specified dates. Pond 12 has only one observed date per evaluable interval; within-interval linearity cannot be tested. Pond 10 also has sparse, irregular early dates; its 4.29e-6 g/day early departure is small enough to be explained by the stored decimal precision, unlike the larger anomalies in other ponds. Pond 11 contains repeated constant labels, multiple weights on four shifted dates, and dates through 2022-02-07, beyond the specified grid. Its constant stretches do not demonstrate a 14-day interpolation scheme.

## Data exceptions

Pond 01 has two missing weight/length labels. Pond 04 has a nonnumeric length label `Jan-00`, treated as missing for numeric length modes. Pond 06 has one missing timestamp, excluded from date grouping but retained for file-order weight-change counts. Multiweight days use their modal weight; their counts are reported above and their dates are in codex_daily_weights.csv. Missing grid dates prevent direct checks against those anchors; absent dates are not evidence against the fixed sampling grid. The final grid row has no next interval, so its increment statistics are blank.
